# Runtime art integration review

Scope: visual inspection of `docs/evidence/runtime-demo.mp4` sample sheets and the named screenshots, captured from build `0213c2ae1bac3a819738820324fe7dae4d00067e` according to `docs/evidence/playthrough-m1/session-result.json`. The edited clip uses the five source intervals listed in `clip-edit.json` at 1× speed. Later changes are outside this review.

## Verdict

The recorded slice displays the original full-body Sword character and recognizable directional, attack, and death poses in the game world. No conspicuous broken body, missing limb, or permanent character aura appears in the inspected neutral and action samples. The evidence does **not** establish a clearly readable alternate side-walk foot cycle, a stable foot pivot throughout animation, or a visibly articulated revival sequence. Treat these as visual verification gaps rather than proven runtime failures; inspect a close gameplay loop and an unobscured revival before claiming those specific requirements are accepted.

## Evidence and observations

- `playthrough-m1/neutral-sword.png`: the centered, front-facing Sword has a visible face, dark topknot, blue-gray robe, legs, and sword; no persistent aura surrounds the idle character. The lanterns, grass, stone path, NPC and hall share the muted cultivation palette.
- `playthrough-m1/runtime-pose-samples.png`: clip 8.8–9.4 s and 12.0–12.3 s show front-facing poses; 9.7–10.6 s show the back; 11.0–11.3 s show the left profile; and 13.0–13.6 s show the right profile. The facing changes are visually distinguishable and the face is visible in the front and profile views at this sampled scale. The 14.0 s sample shows a low sword-extended attack; 16.4 s shows the skill effect near the figure, but this isolated image does not establish the complete casting motion. The 15.2 s sample includes dodge text, but the sampled body pose alone is insufficient to verify the full dodge animation.
- Side walking at 11.0–11.3 s and 13.0–13.3 s produces distinct stride/standing silhouettes. At gameplay scale the overlapping dark legs and robe prevent reliable identification of alternate leading feet. This agrees with the earlier source review: clear stride and passing poses, but opposite-foot ordering is not certain. The sampled character appears grounded on the paths, with no obvious gross displacement; sparse frames and a moving world camera cannot establish a stable world-space foot pivot or rule out foot sliding.
- `runtime-pose-samples.png` clip 67.0 s shows a lowered figure and 68.0 s a prone body with weapon. `respawn-camera-samples.png` 68.5 s shows the prone figure at zero health; by 69.0 s health is restored and the character is at the safe-point portal. This supports an observed death and return, but the portal ring overlays the small character and the sampled frames do not resolve a distinct kneel-to-standing respawn animation. The ring belongs to the visible portal context and is not evidence of a permanent character aura.
- `north-portal-visible.png`, `herbalist-foundation.png`, and `barracks-foundation.png` show the hall, herbalist, forge, NPCs, paths and portal in a coherent setting; the player remains recognizable next to NPCs. In the herbalist screenshot the building partially obscures the NPC's name label, a localized UI/occlusion issue, not a body-art blocker. `art-integration-smoke/forest-1920x1080.png` shows the Sword, snake, wolves, rocks and trees coexisting in the forest. The repeated tree placements and strong portal rings are visually apparent but do not prevent scene recognition.

## Limits

These are selected captured frames, not a fresh game run or a frame-by-frame measurement. They do not prove animation smoothness, FPS, audio, complete input responsiveness, every directional action, consistent pivot over time, or all possible occlusion cases. Runtime performance thresholds are separately measured and outside this visual review. Suggested follow-up: capture a tightly cropped side-walk cycle against a fixed ground reference in both directions, and capture the full revival transition away from the portal overlay if feasible.
